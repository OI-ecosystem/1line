What it does

From frustration to a complaint that lands.

When a company lets you down, the hardest part is knowing what to write and where to go next. 1LINE Complain takes one paragraph about what happened, rates how strong your complaint is and shows what’s missing — then builds the complete strategy: the letter, the timeline, the deadline and the escalation route.

Flights, faulty goods, energy bills, landlords, banks, broadband. Every letter is built from your own account of what happened — factual, firm and ready to send.

Example input

“My airline cancelled my flight 3 hours before departure and refused to rebook me or offer a refund.”
