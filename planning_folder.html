What it does

Portals show what’s for sale. This shows where planning is moving.

What it looks for

Four ways planning creates value.

Brownfield & register land

Sites the council has already recognised as suitable for homes — some still waiting for permission.

Permissions to check

Permissions over three years old, flagged so you can check whether they started, stalled or lapsed.

Conversion & change of use

Smaller sites that suit conversion briefs — with Article 4 restrictions shown where the council publishes them.

Catalysts & the local plan

Recorded infrastructure projects and where the council’s local plan sits in its cycle.
